What he does in a race

Ideas for his job on the track. He never races himself. He floats above the road and makes pushing each other off even wilder.

Main moveBass dropHe floats ahead and slams the road. A shockwave rolls across the lanes and shoves every critter sideways, toward the edge. Hold your line or bump someone into it.
Keeps it closeHaunts the leaderHe picks on whoever is in first place: floats in front of them, blocks a lane, drops the next beat on them. Racers at the back get a chance to catch up.
ChaosWrong songNow and then he swaps the music, and for a few seconds left and right are flipped for everyone. Great for clips, terrible for whoever is near the edge.
