  • The flame burns downBlaze's flame shrinks every second. Grab embers to relight it. When it goes out, the run is over.
  • Tricks build a comboHoops, smashes, loops, flings and tunes push your combo up to x8. Your score grows faster with every step.

The ten pieces

Each piece gives you a choice: the risky way has more embers or a trick, the safe way keeps your combo. Round 1 pieces fit the current game quickly; Round 2 pieces need new track shapes. Click a card to play that piece on its own.
